Summary

goodshorts is a private desktop tool that uploads and schedules videos on its owner's own YouTube channels. It has no server: everything runs on the owner's computer, and data goes only to YouTube (Google). Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Information goodshorts accesses

When you sign in with Google, goodshorts asks for two YouTube permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload - to upload the videos you choose and set their title, description, tags, category, language and scheduled publish time.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly - to read your channel's name and ID, and the publish times of your recent uploads, so new videos are not scheduled on top of existing ones.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl - to update the title, description, tags and language of videos goodshorts uploaded (for example, to fix a description before a scheduled video goes live). goodshorts never deletes videos, comments or ratings.

goodshorts does not access your email, contacts, files, comments, subscribers, analytics or payment data.

How the information is used

  • Only to upload and schedule videos on your own channel and to confirm the result to you.
  • Not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI or machine-learning models.
  • Not sold, rented or transferred to anyone.

Storage and security

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the computer where goodshorts runs, in files readable only by that computer's user account. They are never sent anywhere except to Google to refresh access.
  • goodshorts keeps a small local record of what it uploaded (video ID, channel, scheduled time).
  • Videos and their details are sent directly to YouTube over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you remove them. You can revoke goodshorts' access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops goodshorts from reaching your channel; the local token files can then be deleted. Videos already uploaded stay on your channel and are managed in YouTube Studio.

Google API Services User Data Policy

goodshorts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

goodshorts uses YouTube API Services. By using goodshorts you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
