goodshorts

A private upload and scheduling tool for the owner's own YouTube channels.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

What it does

  • Uploads finished short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, category, language and scheduled publish time.
  • Reads the channel's name and recent upload schedule so two videos are never scheduled at the same time.

Who uses it

goodshorts is run by its developer on their own computer, signed in only with their own Google accounts. It is not offered as a public service, and there is no sign-up.

Google access it requests

  • youtube.upload - to upload videos and set their details and schedule.
  • youtube.readonly - to read the channel's name and the publish times of its recent uploads.

Details on how this data is handled are in the Privacy Policy.

goodshorts uses YouTube API Services. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by YouTube or Google.
